Sogrim Hashbon is a Hebrew group-expense calculator for friends. It does not hold funds, connect to bank accounts, or process money transfers.

Review flow:
1. Sign in with the review account supplied in App Review Information.
2. Tap "אירוע חדש" and choose "יציאה רגילה".
3. Add participants and create an expense.
4. Select who paid and who participated in that expense.
5. Open "סיכום" to see the optimized settlement transfers.
6. Open the sharing screen to inspect the invitation link and QR code.
7. Account deletion is available under Profile > "מחיקת חשבון".

The first iOS release contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. Push notifications are not enabled in this release. Once verified, Sign in with Apple is available alongside Google and email authentication.
