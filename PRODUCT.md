# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends and small recurring groups who go out together, split taxis, food, drinks, tickets, and ad hoc purchases, then need a clear personal screen that remembers who they are and what events belong to them.

## Product Purpose

Sogrim Hashbon helps a group enter who paid, who participated in each expense, and which guests joined a specific event. It calculates the weighted result and suggests fewer, smarter transfers, while keeping each user's view personal, remembered, and easy to share by link or QR.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, polished. The product should feel serious enough to trust with money, but simple enough for friends to use without explanation.

## Anti-references

Avoid student-project screens, old web-app styling, crowded dashboards, repeated action panels, invented demo people, visible internal/beta wording, and decorative UI that makes the task harder.

## Design Principles

- Start from the user's next action: create an event, join an event, add an expense, or settle.
- Every screen should answer where am I, what can I do now, and what changed.
- Personal memory is core: the product should remember the user and only show relevant history.
- Prefer fewer high-confidence actions over repeated buttons in several places.
- Make money flows calm and inspectable, with clear names, totals, participants, and transfer status.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface is RTL-first, Hebrew-first, mobile-first, and should maintain readable contrast, large touch targets, reduced-motion support, and clear focus states. Google sign-in can later strengthen identity without blocking guest usage.
